# Communication / Email Clients

- [Airmail](airmail.md) — Fast email client. For both Mac OS and iOS.
- [CanaryMail](canarymail.md) — Secure email app for Mac and iPhone with built-in PGP Support and AI assistance.
- [ElectronMail](electronmail.md) — An Electron-based unofficial desktop client for ProtonMail.
- [Foxmail](foxmail.md) — Fast email client.
- [MailTags](mailtags.md) — Use tags to organize email and schedule.
- [Mailspring](mailspring.md) — A beautiful, fast, and fully open source mail client.
- [N1](n1.md) — Extensible, open-source mail app, free for developers and $7/month for Pro.
- [Nylas Mail](nylas-mail.md) — Extensible desktop mail app built on the modern web.
- [Polymail](polymail.md) — Simple, beautiful and powerful email client.
- [Postbox](postbox.md) — Powerful, simple and beautiful email client, need to pay for a license.
- [Spark](spark.md) — Fast email client. For both Mac OS and iOS.
- [ThunderBird](thunderbird.md) — Software that makes email easier.
- [Tutanota](tutanota.md) — Encrypted email focused on security and privacy.
- [Edison Mail](edison-mail.md) — A customisable, simple, and beautiful email client.
- [Skiff Mail](skiff-mail.md) — Encrypted & Decentralized Email -- available on web, iOS/Android, and macOS.
