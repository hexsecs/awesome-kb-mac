# Voice-to-Text

- [Aiko](aiko.md) — High-quality on-device transcription.
- [Amical](amical.md) — Open-source AI dictation and note-taking app that types into any app and formats the text for the app you are in, using local Whisper or Parakeet models.
- [buzz](buzz.md) — Transcribes and translates audio offline on your personal computer. Powered by OpenAI's Whisper.
- [EnviousWispr](enviouswispr.md) — Sub-second dictation running Whisper/Parakeet locally with privacy-first AI text polish.
- [FnKey](fnkey.md) — Push-to-talk voice input tool that pastes transcribed text instantly.
- [Google AI Edge Eloquent](google-ai-edge-eloquent.md) — Free on-device AI dictation and voice editing from Google that works in any app, currently English only.
- [inlaut](inlaut.md) — Fully local push-to-talk dictation for German and English, running a Parakeet model on-device and typing at the cursor.
- [LocalScribe](localscribe.md) — Open-source local transcription and subtitle editor for microphones, media files, and Mac audio.
- [OpenDictation](opendictation.md) — Open-source dictation utility with local and cloud speech-to-text.
- [OpenQuack](openquack.md) — Privacy-first voice dictation tool that transcribes locally with WhisperKit and inserts text at the cursor with a hotkey.
- [OpenTypeless](opentypeless.md) — Open-source AI voice input that types polished text into any app.
- [Ottex](ottex.md) — AI dictation tool that formats text for the app or site you are using.
- [ParrotFlow](parrotflow.md) — Local dictation app that you can program with your rules, scripts, and vocabulary.
- [Presspeech](presspeech.md) — Native push-to-talk local dictation app for Apple Silicon Macs using Parakeet TDT v3 (CoreML/ANE).
- [Spokenly](spokenly.md) — Voice-to-text with 100+ languages, offline mode, and AI-powered formatting.
- [Tambourine](tambourine.md) — Open-source, customizable AI voice dictation that works in any app.
- [Tamka](tamka.md) — Open-source on-device dictation that runs NVIDIA Parakeet models on the Neural Engine and types at the cursor in any app.
- [TypeWhisper](typewhisper.md) — Local Whisper-based speech-to-text with a global hotkey.
- [VoiceInk](voiceink.md) — Real-time speech-to-text app.
- [Voltip](voltip.md) — Open-source push-to-talk dictation with on-device or cloud recognition, AI polish, and a paired Android phone as an optional microphone.
- [VoxFlow](voxflow.md) — Open-source voice input workspace with local and cloud ASR, OCR, history, and coding-agent workflows.
- [Voxt](voxt.md) — Hold-to-talk voice input tool with AI transcription rules per app and URL, and built-in translation.
- [WhisperDrop 2](whisperdrop-2.md) — Open-source local transcription for audio, video, YouTube, and meetings, with dictation into any app.
- [Whispering](whispering.md) — Multi-provider speech-to-text with AI transformations and keyboard shortcuts.
- [Willow Voice](willow-voice.md) — AI dictation with automatic editing, style-matching, and noise optimization.
