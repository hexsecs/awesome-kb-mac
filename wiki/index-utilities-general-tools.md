# Utilities / General Tools

- [AerialSwap](aerialswap.md) — Turn any video into a native Aerial wallpaper for the lock screen and desktop, in its own group in System Settings.
- [AirServer](airserver.md) — Most advanced screen mirroring software receiver for Mac, PC and Xbox One.
- [Cadran](cadran.md) — Renders 22 customizable clock faces on your Mac desktop wallpaper and screensaver.
- [DNS Optimizer](dns-optimizer.md) — A DNS changer and performance‑benchmarking tool for Apple devices (macOS & iOS).
- [DevKnife](devknife.md) — A native Mac app for dozens of daily dev tasks, from network scans to JSON formatting.
- [DevToysMac](devtoysmac.md) — Offline toolbox that helps developers in daily tasks.
- [DevUtils.app](devutils-app.md) — Developer toolbox for formatting, converting, and debugging everyday data.
- [Deskmark](deskmark.md) — Add watermarks to the desktop, ideal for recording videos.
- [Etcher](etcher.md) — Flash OS images to SD cards & USB drives, safely and easily.
- [Equinox](equinox.md) — Create dynamic wallpapers for macOS.
- [HTTrack](httrack.md) — Useful tool for downloading a whole website and offline browsing.
- [Latest](latest.md) — A tiny app that checks if all your apps from any source are up to date.
- [Liftoff](liftoff.md) — Open-source Launchpad replacement with live window previews, window-title search, and one-click folder organizing.
- [Lucid](lucid.md) — Live 4K wallpapers with moving space, nature and ocean loops on every display, plus Apple Aerials.
- [Lungo](lungo.md) — Prevent your Mac from going to sleep.
- [LaunchNext](launchnext.md) — Classic Launchpad experience, relive old macOS.
- [lo-rain](lo-rain.md) — Create a customizable rain over your desktop and apps, with splash over the dock.
- [Memo](memo.md) — Simple and elegant app. Unlock memos even more quickly using Touch ID.
- [Nifro](nifro.md) — Turn websites into configurable desktop wallpapers, with separate views for each display.
- [Numi](numi.md) — Beautiful calculator app for Mac.
- [NextDNS](nextdns.md) — The new firewall for the modern Internet.
- [OpenDisplay](opendisplay.md) — Use a spare iPhone or iPad as a second display for your Mac, wired (USB) or over WiFi, with touch input.
- [Panoply](panoply.md) — Free file tools that run on your computer: PDF, images, video and audio, OCR, ID photos, instant file search and a disk map.
- [Phosphene](phosphene.md) — Use any video as your macOS wallpaper on the desktop and the lock screen, picked directly from System Settings.
- [MagicWall](magicwall.md) — Set local videos as desktop wallpapers.
- [Plash](plash.md) — Make any website your desktop wallpaper.
- [rem](rem.md) — An open source approach to locally record and enable searching everything you view on your Mac.
- [Rewind](rewind.md) — Searchable history of your screen and audio activity.
- [SlowQuitApps](slowquitapps.md) — An OS X app that adds a global delay of 1 second to the Cmd-Q shortcut.
- [Speediness](speediness.md) — Check your internet speed.
- [SATF](satf.md) — Shake a file while dragging it to convert, merge into a PDF, zip, or extract it from a radial menu under your cursor.
- [Swivel](swivel.md) — Tap a key mid-drag and drop files on a wheel to convert, crop, compress, strip metadata or share them, fully offline.
- [Ultra TabSaver](ultra-tabsaver.md) — The Open Source Tab Manager for Safari
- [Upscayl](upscayl.md) — Free and open-source AI image upscaling tool.
- [Uptick](uptick.md) — Open-source app updater that finds updates from the App Store, Sparkle, Electron feeds and Homebrew, and verifies each download before installing.
- [Vidwall](vidwall.md) — Easily import MP4/MOV videos as system wallpapers and lock screen animations.
- [WaifuX](waifux.md) — Open-source ACG app for wallpapers, animated backgrounds, and anime videos.
- [CapsLockNoDelay](capslocknodelay.md) — Removes caps-lock key activation delay for fast typers.
- [Seodisias](seodisias.md) — SEO crawler that audits sites for technical issues and optimization opportunities.
