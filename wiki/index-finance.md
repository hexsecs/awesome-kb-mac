# Finance

- [HTS for Mac](hts-for-mac.md) — Read-only Korean and US stock workspace in the style of Windows HTS apps, with a 10-level order book, four-chart layouts, watchlists and saved workspaces…
- [Posnic](posnic.md) — Offline-first open-source POS and billing software for retail shops and restaurants.
- [Pulse](pulse-2.md) — Native menu bar market tracker for US, Hong Kong and China stocks, crypto, indices, ETFs and portfolio P&L.
- [SubManager](submanager.md) — Subscription tracker with renewal reminders.
- [SubList](sublist.md) — Track subscriptions, renewals, and spending in one place with reminders, analytics, and iCloud sync.
- [StockDock](stockdock.md) — Menu bar app for real-time stocks, ETFs, crypto and portfolio P&L. Privacy-first, no account, multi-currency.
- [MRRDock](mrrdock.md) — Menu bar app that adds up your MRR from Stripe, RevenueCat, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments and Gumroad into one total. Multi-currency, keys…
- [Moony](moony.md) — Personal finance app for tracking accounts, investments, crypto, property and loans in an encrypted local database, with no account, no cloud and no telemetry.
