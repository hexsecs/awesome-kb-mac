# Reading and Writing Tools / RSS

- [Elytra](elytra.md) — A Simple & Private RSS Feed Reader with native rendering for macOS & iOS.
- [Feedy](feedy.md) — An elegant and lightweight RSS client and news reader for your Mac.
- [Folo](folo.md) — 🧡 Next generation information browser.
- [Leaf](leaf.md) — A news reader for managing subscriptions and enjoying daily news.
- [NetNewsWire](netnewswire.md) — It’s a free and open source feed reader for macOS.
- [Doughnut](doughnut.md) — Beautiful, open-source podcast catcher for Mac.
- [Papr](papr.md) — RSS reader with adjustable typography, keyboard-driven navigation, and highlight export to Readwise, Notion and Obsidian.
- [ReadKit](readkit.md) — Bookmark and RSS management client.
- [Reeder 5](reeder-5.md) — News reader for Feedbin, Feedly, Feed Wrangler and so on.
- [Saga Reader](saga-reader.md) — AI-powered web and RSS reader.
- [Unread](unread.md) — RSS reader with beautiful typography that supports Feedbin, Feedly and so on.
- [Vienna](vienna.md) — RSS/Atom reader for Mac OS X.
