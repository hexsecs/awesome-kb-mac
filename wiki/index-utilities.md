# Utilities

- [Convertino](convertino.md) — Drag a file, tap Shift and drop it on a radial menu to convert or edit it.
- [DNS Easy Switcher](dns-easy-switcher.md) — Menu bar tool for switching DNS providers or custom resolvers.
- [Dropshelf](dropshelf.md) — A drag and drop helper app for macOS.
- [Dropover](dropover.md) — Temporary shelf for collecting and moving dragged files, text, and links.
- [cmd+x](cmd-x.md) — Launch Activity Monitor with Ctrl+Opt+Delete, cut and move files in Finder with Cmd+X.
