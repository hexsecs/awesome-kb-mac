# Audio and Video Tools / Audio Record and Process

- [akou](akou.md) — Open-source call and meeting recorder that transcribes on-device with speaker labels and lets Claude Code or Codex question a call over MCP.
- [CapSoftware](capsoftware.md) — An open-source alternative to Loom. Beautiful, shareable screen recording tool.
- [Carbon](carbon.md) — Menu bar app that transcribes meetings on device and saves each one as a Markdown file, with no bot and no cloud.
- [Digisensus Recorder](digisensus-recorder.md) — Call recorder and bot-free AI meeting note taker for Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime, WhatsApp and phone calls.
- [GarageBand](garageband.md) — Digital audio workstation for recording and music production.
- [jdsp4mac](jdsp4mac.md) — Menu bar app that runs JamesDSP audio effects, including EQ, AutoEQ, convolver, crossfeed and ViPER-DDC, on all system audio.
- [Local Transcriber](local-transcriber.md) — Menu bar app that records calls and meetings and transcribes them on-device with Whisper, with speaker recognition, call detection and dictation into any app.
- [Logic Pro X](logic-pro-x.md) — Professional digital audio workstation for music and audio production.
- [PitchTap](pitchtap.md) — Open-source menu bar app that changes the key of any app's audio live without changing the tempo.
- [Segue](segue.md) — Broadcast audio playout with crossfade, trim, ramp timers, and pause beds for live radio and podcasts.
- [Shiyin](shiyin.md) — Open-source meeting recorder that transcribes on-device, identifies speakers, and writes timestamped notes with your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription.
- [Stargate DAW](stargate-daw.md) — An all-in-one digital audio workstation (DAW) and plugin suite.
- [SystemEQ for Mac](systemeq-for-mac.md) — Free, open-source system-wide parametric equalizer with AutoEQ presets, hearing calibration, and real-time visualization.
- [Tingo](tingo.md) — Open-source meeting notes app that records any call, transcribes it and tells speakers apart on device, and writes minutes whose every point links back to…
- [Utter](utter.md) — On-device voice dictation and meeting transcription powered by whisper.cpp and Metal.
- [Vauta](vauta.md) — Open-source meeting notes app that transcribes any call on device, tells speakers apart and writes cited minutes with Apple Intelligence or your own local…
- [yovoice](yovoice.md) — Local voice creation studio with text-to-speech, voice cloning, emotion control, song generation and MIDI scoring, all running on-device.
