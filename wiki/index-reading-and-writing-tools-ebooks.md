# Reading and Writing Tools / Ebooks

- [Calibre](calibre.md) — Free, open-source e-book manager and reader.
- [BookShelves](bookshelves.md) — Ebook reader and library manager with public-domain books and sync.
- [Clearview](clearview.md) — Tabbed style e-book reader for PDF, EPUB (DRM free), CHM, and MOBI.
- [Comic Commander](comic-commander.md) — Comic library browser and reader for CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT, and PDF files.
- [iChm](ichm.md) — Ebook reader for CHM (Microsoft Compiled HTML help) files.
- [Kindle App](kindle-app.md) — Amazon official reading app of kindle.
- [Klib](klib.md) — New way to manage highlights for Kindle and iBooks.
- [Koodo Reader](koodo-reader.md) — All-in-one eBook reader supporting over 15 formats.
- [Rapid Reader](rapid-reader.md) — Open-source RSVP speed reader for EPUB, PDF, and web articles with a full-text view.
- [Readest](readest.md) — Open-source ebook reader with a clean interface and powerful reading tools.
- [ReadAny](readany.md) — AI-powered e-book reader with semantic search, chat, and note management.
- [Scribus](scribus.md) — Professional layout and publishing software.
- [Sigil](sigil.md) — Multi-platform EPUB ebook Editor.
- [Simple Comic](simple-comic.md) — EBook reader for PDF, CBZ, and CBR formats with Live Text search.
