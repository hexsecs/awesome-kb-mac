# Utilities / Quality of Life Improvements

- [Luminescent](macvirtue.md) — Bring back Keyboard Backlight Shortcuts for the MacBook.
- [KeyCastr](keycastr.md) — Open-source keystroke visualizer.
- [Actions](actions.md) — Provides many useful actions for the Shortcuts app.
- [AI Actions](ai-actions.md) — AI actions for the Shortcuts app.
- [Crisp](crisp.md) — Manage external displays from the menu bar: HiDPI scaling, DDC brightness, color, and presets.
- [DisplayBuddy](displaybuddy.md) — Control external display brightness, contrast, and input source.
- [Dimly](dimly.md) — Control brightness across mixed monitor setups from one menu bar app.
- [f.lux](f-lux.md) — Makes the color of your computer's display adapt to the time of day.
- [Grayscale Mode](grayscale-mode.md) — Toggle a grayscale screen filter from the menu bar or a shortcut.
- [Hyperduck](hyperduck.md) — Receive links from your iOS & visionOS devices.
- [MaCursor](macursor.md) — Custom cursor themes for macOS.
- [Lunar](lunar.md) — Help you adujst brightness, contrast and volumn of your external display.
- [Safelight](safelight.md) — Open-source menu bar app that schedules the color filter, brightness, and Light/Dark appearance, fading the screen to deep red at night.
- [Shifty](shifty.md) — A macOS menu bar app that gives you more control over Night Shift.
- [Snap](snap.md) — Launch an app in a snap. Ridiculously easy shortcut management.
- [Shareful](shareful.md) — Supercharge the system share menu with copy, save, and open actions.
- [Mouse Jiggler for Mac](mouse-jiggler-for-mac.md) — Prevent Mac from sleep with Mac Mouse Mover.
